The Energy Flow

Your Roof. Your Power Plant.

See how seamlessly Solaris turns raw solar energy into power that charges your home, appliances, and electric vehicles.

1. The Sun

Constant source of nuclear fusion energy emitting millions of watts onto earth.

2. Panels

Monocrystalline panels capture photons, triggering DC electricity flow.

3. Your Home

Sleek inverters convert DC to AC electricity, powering household loads.

4. Storage

Excess generation is stored in smart batteries for night time or outages.

5. Grid Feed

Net metering feeds surplus electricity back into the grid for billing credits.
